Cardiac distress and chest pain protocol. Signs: very high heart rate, chest pain, sweating, shortness of breath, collapse with no movement. Steps: keep the person calm and seated or lying with head raised. Loosen tight clothing. Check breathing and pulse. If unresponsive and not breathing normally, start CPR with 30 chest compressions then 2 breaths. If an aspirin is available and the person is conscious with no allergy, one tablet may be chewed. Call for urgent medical evacuation.
